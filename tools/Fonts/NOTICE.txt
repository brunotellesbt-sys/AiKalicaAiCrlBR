Compatibility fonts generated from Carlito and Liberation, licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. Original copyright and license texts accompany these files.

Modified by this project: font family/name records renamed to aliases required by WPF applications under Wine. Glyph outlines are unchanged. These files are NOT Microsoft Calibri, Arial, Segoe UI, Tahoma, MS Sans Serif, Consolas, Courier New, or Times New Roman fonts. The names are compatibility aliases, not claims of origin or endorsement. Original reserved family names are not used as the modified family names.

create_aliases.py documents the transformation, using the fonts supplied by the Debian fonts-crosextra-carlito and fonts-liberation packages. Font source projects: https://github.com/googlefonts/carlito and https://github.com/liberationfonts/liberation-fonts .
