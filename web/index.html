Pokémon Journey

World Alpha 1 · Kanto, Hoenn and Sevii connected by sea.

Choose your starting city, meet your family, explore both regions and travel through Lavender Port.

Playable alpha. Start a new game; saves from earlier LeafGreen releases are incompatible. Full campaigns and balance are still being tested.

Download .gba

Starting region: the title begins in Hoenn. Press Select on the title screen to switch to Kanto, before opening the game menu. Select is Shift on a keyboard. Choose your city before arriving by truck or boat.

Controls: arrows to move, X = A, Z = B, Enter = Start, Shift = Select. On mobile, use the on-screen controls.

Talk to your family for Surf, Dive and Waterfall. Save through the game's menu and export a save backup through the emulator menu. Clearing browser data can delete stored saves.

Open Start → MAP in the game for the integrated world map and your current location. The web atlas and layout are planning references.
